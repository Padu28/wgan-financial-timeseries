Pick a moment in the test period. The generator sees only the last three days and draws thousands of possible futures; the fan shows where most of them land. Then reveal what actually happened.

Does it behave like a real market?

Real asset returns share a handful of statistical habits, known as stylized facts. A useful generator has to reproduce all of them at once, not just the average move.

Can you tell which one is real?

Can you trust its risk numbers?

Out-of-sample checks on the test period, which the model never saw. A calibrated generator's 90% band should contain the outcome 90% of the time.

Watch it learn

Replay training. The generator starts from noise; drag the slider or press play to see the same four scenarios sharpen while the critic's Wasserstein estimate and gradient norm settle.

Under the hood

A conditional Wasserstein GAN with gradient penalty, trained on transformed return windows. Everything here is computed by the bundled pipeline; nothing is hand-picked.
